About NAD Employees Union

Mission

To provide NAD Employees Union members with a simple, reliable digital platform for union administration — making union operations transparent, efficient, and accessible to all members.

Vision

To promote unity, welfare, dignity and legitimate interests of employees through democratic, transparent and constructive representation.

Objectives

Safeguard the rights and interests of all NAD employees. Promote cooperation and solidarity among members. Represent employee grievances to management and government authorities.
